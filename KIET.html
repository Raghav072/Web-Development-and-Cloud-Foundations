About KIET

KIET Deemed To Be University is an educational institution located in Delhi-NCR, Ghaziabad. Established in 1998, the university offers programmes in engineering, computer applications, management, pharmacy and allied disciplines.

KIET focuses on academic excellence, innovation, research, practical learning and industry-oriented education.

Why Choose KIET?

KIET provides students with opportunities for practical learning, technical projects, research, innovation and industry exposure. The university also provides industry-integrated learning tracks in areas such as Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Cloud Computing, Cyber Security, Full Stack Development, Robotics and Semiconductor Design.
